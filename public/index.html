Our Story

Tulia Amboseli Safari Camp

Tulia Amboseli Safari Camp is an eco-friendly safari camp just outside of Kimana Gate in the southeast area of the Amboseli National Park in Kenya. As a small, tented camp, Tulia offers an intimacy like no other camp in the area. Each tent opens to Mt Kilimanjaro and offers wonderful views of both sunrise and sunset. The welcoming restaurant and lounge overlook a very active waterhole where daily visits from elephant, giraffe, zebra, eland, cheetah, lion, wildebeest, warthog, gazelle, waterbuck, and perhaps gerenuk if you are lucky, offers guests an up close, intimate experience. Enjoy the African Sunset during our daily sundowners at the campfire and reminisce about your day’s safari with other guests.
